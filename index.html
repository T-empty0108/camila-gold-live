Camila Gold Live

Camila Gold Live is a YouTube channel (@CamilaGoldLive) that publishes a live XAUUSD (gold) market stream and short daily technical-analysis videos in Thai, with English subtitles.

Camila Gold Live Publisher is the channel's internal tool. The channel owner uses it to upload the channel's own daily videos to YouTube through the YouTube Data API. The tool is not offered to the public, and no other users sign in to it.

Content is personal market commentary for education only and is not financial advice.
