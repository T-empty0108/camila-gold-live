Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

This policy covers Camila Gold Live Publisher (the "App"), the internal tool the Camila Gold Live channel uses to upload its own videos to YouTube. Only the channel owner signs in to it.

Data the App accesses

  • When the channel owner signs in with Google, the App asks for the YouTube scopes youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl.
  • The App uses them to upload videos, set their title, description, privacy and thumbnail, add captions, and read the name of the signed-in channel.
  • The App does not read, collect or store data about YouTube viewers or any other users.

How data is stored

The OAuth refresh token is stored only on the channel owner's own computer. It is never sent to any server other than Google's.

Sharing

The App does not sell, rent, share or transfer Google user data to any third party. It does not use Google user data for advertising.

Limited Use

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. When access is revoked, the stored token is deleted.
